Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

SpeakUp Language Center ("we", "our", or "us") operates an AI-powered chatbot service on Facebook Messenger that assists parents and students with course inquiries and trial class registration. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our chatbot.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our chatbot via Facebook Messenger, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Your Facebook Messenger user ID (provided by Meta's platform)
  • Messages and interactions you send to our chatbot
  • Student information you voluntarily provide (such as name, age, and preferred class schedule) for the purpose of trial class registration
  • Contact information such as phone number or email address, if provided

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data exclusively to:

  • Respond to course inquiries and provide information about our language programs
  • Facilitate trial class bookings and student registration
  • Send reminder notifications about upcoming trial classes
  • Improve chatbot performance and accuracy

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to our language school services.

3. Facebook Messenger and Meta Platform Data

Our chatbot operates on Facebook Messenger using Meta's pages_messaging permission. Through this integration, we receive your Messenger user ID and the content of messages you send to our Page. This data is used solely to deliver automated responses to your inquiries. We do not access any other Facebook profile data, friends list, or personal information beyond what is necessary to operate the chatbot.

Your use of Facebook Messenger is also governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain conversation data for up to thirty (30) days. After this period, data is automatically deleted from our systems. Registration information related to enrolled students may be retained for the duration of their enrollment.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties. Data is only shared when strictly necessary to operate our services, such as processing course registrations with our internal school management system.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our chatbot service is operated by parents or guardians on behalf of children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. All interactions are assumed to be conducted by a parent or guardian aged 18 or above.

9. Third-Party Platforms

This chatbot operates on Facebook Messenger, a third-party messaging platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. Your use of that platform is subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the service after changes are posted indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
